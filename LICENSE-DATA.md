## Third-party data — their own terms

Files derived from third-party sources (OECD, OECD-WTO, World Bank, ILO, WHO,
EITI, UNU-WIDER, BLS, CRS, IMF, EIA, BGS, ITU, UN, Tax Foundation, and
others) remain subject to the terms of their original providers, documented
file-by-file in `data/guides/README.md`. They are redistributed here only
where the provider's terms permit; nothing in this repository relicenses
them.

Proprietary inputs (Bureau van Dijk Orbis extracts, and other sources marked
as non-redistributable in `data/guides/README.md`) are **not** included in
this repository and must be obtained under the user's own licence.
